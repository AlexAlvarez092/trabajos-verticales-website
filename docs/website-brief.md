# Website Brief — Evolution Vertical

## Goals

- Establish an online presence that conveys professionalism and credibility.
- Attract new clients (lead generation through direct contact).
- No online quoting or estimates.

## Target audience

General public, without segmentation: residents' associations, property managers,
individuals, and companies.

## Format

Single-page website (landing). A navigation menu links to each section via smooth
in-page scrolling (anchors), without reloading or changing pages.

## Sections

1. Home (hero)
2. Services
3. About us / Why choose us (values: safety, commitment, efficiency)
4. Service area
5. Contact

## Contact method

Direct action buttons: call, WhatsApp, and email. No contact form.

## Languages

Spanish, Valencian, and English.

## Service area

Valencian Community (specific area to be confirmed by the client).
